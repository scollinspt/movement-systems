# Research

This directory is for research questions, study plans, analyses, and outputs arising
from the coordinated course sequence. Connections to the physiology prerequisite and
the broader Physiolog scholarship program should be documented here as they develop.

Movement ontology, engine mappings, computational scenarios, and reproducible model
experiments belong in `movement-models-`; this directory owns the educational and
curricular research questions that motivate or evaluate them. Reviewed outputs can enter
the public site only through the process in
[../docs/workspace-integration.md](../docs/workspace-integration.md) and
[../docs/publication-policy.md](../docs/publication-policy.md).

Do not place identifiable student information or restricted educational records in the
repository.
