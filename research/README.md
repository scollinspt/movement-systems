# Research

This directory is for public HMS Lab and Movement Systems research questions, project
records, methods, approved analyses, and outputs. Research may arise from clinical or
educational observations, methodological problems, theory development, field or
laboratory measurement, industry questions, or computational work.

Use the record structures in
[../docs/content-model.md](../docs/content-model.md) and the lifecycle in
[../docs/research-architecture.md](../docs/research-architecture.md). Raw data,
identifiable information, consent records, confidential partner information, and
unapproved research records belong in governed private storage rather than this public
repository.

Movement ontology, engine mappings, computational scenarios, and reproducible model
experiments belong in `movement-models-`; this directory owns the public scientific
questions and interpretations that motivate or evaluate them. Reviewed outputs can enter
the public site only through the process in
[../docs/workspace-integration.md](../docs/workspace-integration.md) and
[../docs/publication-policy.md](../docs/publication-policy.md).

Do not place identifiable student information or restricted educational records in the
repository.
