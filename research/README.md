# Research

This directory is for public HMS Lab and Movement Systems research questions, project
records, methods, approved analyses, and outputs. Research may arise from clinical or
educational observations, methodological problems, theory development, field or
laboratory measurement, industry questions, or computational work.

Use the record structures in
[../docs/content-model.md](../docs/content-model.md) and the lifecycle in
[../docs/research-architecture.md](../docs/research-architecture.md). Raw data,
identifiable information, consent records, confidential partner information, and
unapproved research records belong in governed private storage rather than this public
repository.

Movement ontology, engine mappings, computational scenarios, and reproducible model
experiments belong in `movement-models-`; this directory owns the public scientific
questions and interpretations that motivate or evaluate them. Reviewed outputs can enter
the public site only through the process in
[../docs/workspace-integration.md](../docs/workspace-integration.md) and
[../docs/publication-policy.md](../docs/publication-policy.md).

Do not place identifiable student information or restricted educational records in the
repository.

## Proposed Projects

1. [Movesense acquisition and single-sensor feasibility](projects/movesense-opensense-foundation.md)
	establishes governed one-sensor acquisition from non-human bench recording through,
	if permitted, a simple human feasibility session.
2. [Movesense multi-sensor timing and scaling](projects/movesense-multisensor-scaling.md)
	tests two sensors and then staged 3-, 5-, and 10-sensor configurations non-human
	before corresponding governed human feasibility stages.
3. [Movesense calibration and orientation validation](projects/movesense-orientation-validation.md)
	establishes calibration, fusion, coordinate, and orientation limits.
4. [Movesense-OpenSense interoperability](projects/movesense-opensense-interoperability.md)
	develops bounded, reproducible OpenSense calibration and inverse-kinematics acceptance.
5. [Movesense-OpenSense and OpenCap comparison](projects/movesense-opencap-validation.md)
	extends the accepted wearable/OpenSense method with synchronized video comparison.
6. [Ventilatory Pump Measurement and Mechanics](projects/ventilatory-pump-measurement-mechanics.md)
	develops accessible measurement of maximal inspiratory pressure and a foundation for
	studying ventilatory pump function, chest-wall motion, and thoracic mechanics.
