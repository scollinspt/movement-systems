\section*{Appendix A: SFMA Top Tier Movement Definitions}

The following patterns are core to the Selective Functional Movement Assessment (SFMA) system. While abbreviated throughout this manual for consistency, they reflect full-body, multi-joint movement sequences and are central to functional screening and regional interdependence reasoning.

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{MSF — Multisegmental Flexion:} Forward bend assessing symmetric trunk, hip, and hamstring flexibility with lumbar spine reversal.
  \item \textbf{MSE — Multisegmental Extension:} Backward bend assessing hip and spine extension with balance and scapular control.
  \item \textbf{MSR — Multisegmental Rotation:} Trunk rotation with feet fixed, testing pelvic, thoracic, and cervical contributions.
  \item \textbf{SLS — Single Leg Stance:} Postural control, balance, and motor strategy during unilateral stance.
  \item \textbf{ADS — Arms Down Squat:} Modified squat pattern emphasizing lower extremity control without overhead arm compensation.
\end{itemize}

\noindent
These movement labels appear frequently in clinical integration prompts and checklist items. Refer to this glossary as needed to interpret lab objectives and testing rationale.
